Community Project

Street2Home

Street2Home is a QCS project focused on making local resources easier to find and use for people experiencing homelessness or housing instability.

The goal is a welcoming, simple experience that helps people quickly find practical resources such as food, shelter, showers, transportation, IDs, and other local support.
